1. What Housley is

Housley is a family money-management app. One family shares a single "Fund" (a shared pool of money with a budget), logs expenses, tracks petrol, keeps receipts, sets savings goals, plans meals, assigns chores, and can use AI helpers to understand its spending. The App is provided "as is" on Google Play.
