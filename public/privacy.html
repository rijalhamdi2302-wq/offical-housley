Housley — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026 · Last updated: 20 August 2026

Your family's financial data is sensitive. We take this seriously. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how we protect it. If anything is unclear, email us at support@housley.app.

1. What we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account infoEmail, hashed password, your name, family nameTo create and secure your account
Family financial recordsFund top-ups, expenses, amounts, categories, shops, notes, budgets, goals, billsThe core purpose of the App — so your family can track money together
Receipts & photosReceipt photos, profile photos, transfer-proof screenshotsTo keep proof of purchases and personalize profiles
Device & usageBasic logs (IP, timestamps) when you use the APISecurity, rate-limiting, and fixing bugs
Payment recordsPurchase tokens from ToyyibPayTo confirm and verify Pro purchases — never your card number

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. Ever.
  • We do not show ads and do not use ad trackers.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, or other apps' data.
  • We do not read your receipts for marketing. Receipts are used only to serve you (scanning, catalog memory, analytics).

3. How your data is stored

  • Stored on secure cloud servers (MongoDB) hosted by a reputable provider, accessed only over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Passwords and PINs are hashed with bcrypt — even we cannot read them.
  • Sessions use JWT tokens with automatic expiry; biometric unlock uses a rotating device token you can revoke by logging out.
  • Family isolation: each family's data is separated by a family ID; no family can read another family's records.
  • Receipt photos on the free tier stay on your device only; Pro can back them up to the cloud.

4. AI processing (important)

Housley's AI helpers (receipt scanning, meal plans, spending insights, "ask about your money") are powered by a third-party AI service (Groq). When you use an AI feature: the photo or text you send is transmitted to the AI service only for that request, and only if AI is switched on for your family (the provider can switch it off in Settings). We instruct the AI service not to use your data to train their models. AI answers are estimates; always double-check amounts the AI reads from a receipt.

We only share what each partner strictly needs, and we choose partners that keep data confidential.

6. Retention

Your data stays as long as your account is active. If you delete records in the App, they are deleted from our servers. If you delete your account, your family's data is erased within 30 days (except what we must keep by law).

7. Children & family profiles

Housley is a family app. Parents create profiles for their children (roles like "kid / dependent") and control the family's settings. Children's profiles only exist inside a parent-managed family. We do not market to children, show them ads, or collect more data than described above. The person who adds a child's profile confirms they are that child's parent or guardian.

8. Your rights (PDPA & GDPR-style)

You may, at any time: access your data (it's all visible in the App); export it (Excel / PDF); correct it (edit records); delete it (by range, factory reset, or account deletion); object / withdraw consent (switch off AI, or delete your account); and lodge a complaint with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP). Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security incidents

If a data breach ever affects your personal or financial data, we will notify you and the relevant authority within the time required by law, and tell you exactly what happened and what you should do (e.g. change your password).
